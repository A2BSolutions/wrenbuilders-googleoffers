2. What we collect

  • Your contact details: name, mobile number, email address, street address, city or town and postcode.
  • Your survey answers. Kitchen survey: what you are looking for, when you would like it fitted, whether you own the property, and whether it is in Cornwall. Garage survey: what you would like to turn your garage into, the type of garage, whether you own the property, and whether it is in Cornwall.
  • Where you came from: which advert or campaign brought you to the survey (for example the campaign name and a Facebook click ID), the survey page you used, and when you sent it.
  • Technical data collected by our advertising and analytics tools while you use the survey pages. See section 8.

3. How we use your details

For every enquiry we receive and record your details, and check whether your property is in the area we cover.

Property in Cornwall. Your enquiry goes into our sales process. Our team is told by email, your details are added to our lead sheet, an enquiry record is created in our customer system, and we may call or text you about your project.

[[APPROVAL NEEDED: confirm the wording for the call and text contact, how you can stop texts, and how consent for texts is obtained.]]

Property outside Cornwall. We still create or update a record for you in our customer system. It holds your contact details, your survey answers, where you came from, and a label showing the property is outside our area. We do not create a sales opportunity, add it to our lead sheet, notify our sales team or send you a sales text.

[[APPROVAL NEEDED: state why enquiries from outside Cornwall are kept.]]

Measuring our adverts. When a qualifying enquiry is made, we tell Meta (Facebook) that an enquiry happened, so we can measure and improve our adverts. This does not include your name, contact details or survey answers.
