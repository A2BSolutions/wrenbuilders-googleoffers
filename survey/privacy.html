We decide how and why the details you give us in these surveys are used. In data protection terms, we are the controller.

2. What we collect

  • Your contact details: name, mobile number, email address, street address, city or town and postcode.
  • Your survey answers. Kitchen survey: what you are looking for, when you would like it fitted, whether you own the property, and whether it is in Cornwall. Garage survey: what you would like to turn your garage into, the type of garage, whether you own the property, and whether it is in Cornwall.
  • Where you came from: which advert or campaign brought you to the survey (for example the campaign name and a Facebook click ID), the survey page you used, and when you sent it.
  • Technical data from your visit, such as your IP address and device type. Our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs for security and to run the service. The logs of the survey service itself do not contain your name, contact details or answers.
  • Optional cookie data if you accept optional cookies. See section 8.

3. How and why we use your details

The table lists each use and the lawful basis we rely on under UK data protection law.

Enquiry from a property in Cornwall. We record your enquiry and our sales team is told by email. Your details are added to our lead sheet, and an enquiry record is created in our customer system. We may call or text you about your project, so we can give you the quote or home visit you asked for.

Lawful basis: taking steps at your request before a contract, such as giving you a quote (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Where that does not apply, our legitimate interests in answering enquiries made to our business (Article 6(1)(f)).

Enquiry from a property outside Cornwall. We still create or update a record for you in our customer system. It holds your contact details, your survey answers, where you came from, and a label showing the property is outside our area. We do not create a sales opportunity, add it to our lead sheet, notify our sales team or send you a sales text. We keep the record so that we have a note of your enquiry and can deal with it if you get back in touch, and to understand how our adverts are performing.

Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in keeping a record of enquiries we cannot take on and in measuring our adverts (Article 6(1)(f)). You can object at any time. See section 9.

Measuring our adverts. If you accept optional cookies, we use Google and Meta tools to measure our adverts. After a qualifying enquiry (a property in Cornwall), the Meta Pixel tells Meta that an enquiry happened, with the offer and campaign names. It does not include your name, contact details or survey answers. If you reject optional cookies, none of this happens.

Legal basis: your consent. This is also the permission needed under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) to store or read information on your device.

4. Calls and texts

If your property is in Cornwall, we may call or text you about your project. The first text is a reply to your enquiry and is sent automatically by our customer system. We use your number to deal with your enquiry. If we want to send you marketing texts or emails that are not part of dealing with your enquiry, we will ask for your permission first.

If you do not want calls or texts, tell us by replying to a text or by using the details in section 10, and we will stop. We do not call or text people whose property is outside Cornwall about sales.

5. Who we share it with

These providers help us run the survey and handle your enquiry on our behalf. They may use your details only to provide their service to us.

  • GoHighLevel (LeadConnector): our customer system. It holds every enquiry and sends our texts.
  • Google (Google Sheets): our lead sheet, for enquiries in Cornwall.
  • Vercel: hosts the survey pages and passes your submission to our customer system.
  • The agency and IT providers that manage our advertising and systems for us, who may have access to these systems.

If you accept optional cookies, Google and Meta (Facebook) also receive technical data from your visit. They use it under their own privacy policies. We may also share details where the law requires it.

6. Transfers outside the UK

Some of these providers are based in, or process data in, countries outside the UK, including the United States. UK law requires a safeguard for this, such as an adequacy decision (for example the UK-US data bridge for certified US companies) or approved standard contract terms. Ask us using the details in section 10 if you want to know which safeguard applies to a provider.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your details only for as long as we need them for the reasons in section 3. We do not set one period for everything, because it depends on what happens next:

  • Enquiries from Cornwall: while we deal with your enquiry and any project that follows. After that, for as long as we need records for legal, tax, insurance or warranty reasons, or to deal with a complaint or claim.
  • Enquiries from outside Cornwall: for as long as we need the record to deal with you getting back in touch and to understand how our adverts are performing.
  • Your cookie choice is kept on your device for 12 months, then we ask again.

When we no longer need your details, we delete them or remove anything that identifies you. You can ask us to delete them sooner. See section 9.

8. Cookies and similar tools

Essential. The survey stores your cookie choice on your device. It also keeps your answers, campaign details and a one-time marker for a completed enquiry in your browser session, which is cleared when you close the tab. The survey needs these to work, and they are not shared with Google or Meta.

Optional, only if you accept. Google Tag Manager, which runs our Google tags for measuring and improving our adverts, and the Meta Pixel. They can set cookies and collect information such as your IP address, device and the pages you view. Nothing from Google or Meta is requested until you accept. This privacy page never loads them.

Your choice. Use the buttons below at any time. Rejecting stops optional tools loading on later pages and removes the optional cookies we can remove from this site. It does not delete data Google or Meta already hold. You can ask them for that.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you (access);
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your details;
  • restrict how we use them;
  • stop using them for a particular purpose (object), including where we rely on our legitimate interests;
  • where it applies, give you a copy in a portable format; and
  • withdraw your consent for optional cookies at any time (see section 8).

We will reply within one month. To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
